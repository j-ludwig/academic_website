5 Multidimensional Scaling

Work in progress. This chapter will be added during the course.

MDS starts from a table of distances between observations, such as travel times, and rebuilds coordinates whose straight-line distances reproduce them (Section 1.7.3). The ideas of the earlier chapters then apply to those coordinates.
